# Rollable factors: 

## Break in time:
    - Odd is day time
    - Even is night time

## Human interaction 
  1: Wild Card (Judge picks)
  2: Security Guard stops you
  3: IT Stops you 
  4: Staff interaction Bob (Sales or Janitor)
  5: Staff interaction Alice (HR, Accounting, manager) 
  6: Wild Card (Judge picks)

## Amount of Total Questions:
- 1 & 6: 4 total questions per encounter min
- 2 & 5: 5 total questions per encounter min
- 3 & 4: 6 Total questions per encounter min

Side Note:
- Side Note: force the competitor(s) to end a conversation, otherwise you can get them in a loop, and then they can waste time
